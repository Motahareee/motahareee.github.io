Motahare Mounesan Ph.D.

Postdoctoral Researcher · Texas A&M University

I'm Motahare, a computer science researcher passionate about AI, reinforcement learning, and distributed systems at the edge. Today's most capable AI models are too big for the phones, cameras, and sensors where they're needed most, and sending everything to the cloud is slow, expensive, and not always private. My research makes big multimodal models (ones that see, hear, and read) run efficiently on small devices, and uses reward and feedback to teach large language models to keep improving.

Besides building things on a computer, I love nature, capturing whatever makes my eyes sparkle, the discomfort of inversions in yoga 🤸‍♀️, the uphills of Central Park on my bike, and crafting things from time to time.
