// About

I am a postdoctoral researcher specializing in Reinforcement Learning, AI, and Edge Computing. My work is focused on multimodal intelligence at the edge and LLM-assisted reinforcement learning for real-time, context-aware decision-making in dynamic, resource-constrained environments.

I received my PhD from The Graduate Center, CUNY, where I developed frameworks for DNN inference optimization, adversarial robustness in collaborative inference, and RL-driven scheduling for edge-cloud systems.

My current research at Texas A&M explores how large language models can guide reinforcement learning agents to make faster, smarter decisions on IoT devices and edge servers — bringing intelligence closer to where data is generated.
